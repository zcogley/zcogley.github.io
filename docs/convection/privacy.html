Convection Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Convection support · Zac Cogley

Convection is an Apple TV app developed by Charles Zachary Cogley. This policy describes the app’s data handling and the Convection support pages.

Artwork and settings

Convection generates its artwork on your Apple TV. It does not require an account, include advertising or tracking SDKs, or sell your personal information.

Artwork choices, colors, motion settings, and brightness preferences are stored locally on your Apple TV. They are not sent to the developer.

Optional location

If you choose Sunrise / Sunset or Use Location, Convection requests location permission through tvOS. It requests approximate location and rounds the coordinates to two decimal places before storing or using them for weather requests.

Sunrise and sunset calculations run on your Apple TV. This feature alone does not send your coordinates to the developer or a weather service. You can use the artwork and a manual brightness schedule without granting location permission.

Optional weather adjustment

Weather Adjusted is off by default. When enabled with location available, the app sends rounded latitude and longitude to Open-Meteo over HTTPS to request cloud cover. Requests normally refresh every 30 minutes while the feature is active. Open-Meteo also receives the internet connection’s IP address as part of the request. The app saves recent weather information locally so it can use it between updates.

Open-Meteo states that its free API may retain IP addresses for service maintenance and misuse prevention, and request logs containing coordinates for troubleshooting. Its policy says this information is not linked to user identities and that log files are deleted after 90 days. See Open-Meteo’s privacy policy.

These requests are used for app functionality, not advertising or tracking. The developer does not receive the requests or operate a server that collects your location.

Your choices and local data

  • Turn off Weather Adjusted to stop future weather requests.
  • Use Manual timing to run the brightness schedule without sunrise and sunset calculations.
  • Revoke Convection’s location permission in Apple TV Settings to stop location access. The app clears its saved coordinates and weather cache when it next checks location permission for an enabled location-based brightness feature.
  • Deleting Convection from the Apple TV removes the app’s locally stored settings and cached data from that device.

Support and diagnostics

If you email support, the developer receives the email address you use and anything you include in your message. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate the problem. You can ask for your support correspondence to be deleted by contacting support.

Convection includes no third-party analytics or crash-reporting SDK. Apple may provide app analytics or crash diagnostics through its own services, depending on your Apple settings and, for beta builds, TestFlight. Those services are governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

These web pages

The Convection support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages and contain no advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub may process technical request information, such as your IP address, to serve and protect the website. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Other pages and external websites linked here may have different practices.
