Convection

Living art for Apple TV · Support

Privacy policy · Zac Cogley

Convection turns your television into a canvas of flowing color, weaving flocks, and shimmering water. Choose Fluid, Murmuration, or Water, or let Automatic rotate through all three.

Convection requires Apple TV with tvOS 17 or later. It has no account, advertising, or in-app purchases.

Using the Siri Remote

Control What it does
Press the clickpad Open the artwork controls.
Directional controls, then Select Move between options and apply a choice.
Play/Pause Pause or resume the artwork.
Back Close the controls.
New Cycle Start a fresh scene; advance to the next artwork in Automatic mode.

Use the controls to choose colors, adjust motion, and change each artwork’s appearance. Your preferences are saved on your Apple TV.

Brightness and location

Open Day / Night to set separate daytime and nighttime brightness levels. Manual timing works without internet access or location permission.

Sunrise / Sunset uses approximate location to calculate local sun times on your Apple TV. Weather Adjusted is optional and off by default. When enabled with location available, it sends rounded coordinates to Open-Meteo to retrieve cloud cover. See the privacy policy for details.

Common questions

Why is the artwork dark?

Check the daytime and nighttime levels under Day / Night. Fluid also gradually fades as a cycle ends, then starts a new flow. Select New Cycle to begin again immediately.

Does it work offline?

The artwork and manual brightness schedule work offline. Sunrise / Sunset needs location permission to obtain your approximate location; sun times are then calculated locally. Weather Adjusted needs an internet connection. If weather data becomes unavailable, the app falls back toward the selected daytime brightness.

Is there sound?

Convection does not play audio.

Does it replace the Apple TV screen saver?

Convection runs as an app. Keep it open to enjoy the artwork; it keeps the display awake while active.

Something is not working

Close and reopen Convection, and check that the app and tvOS are up to date. If the issue continues, contact support with your Apple TV model, tvOS version, app version, and the steps that led to the problem. A photo or short video can help; avoid including private information.
